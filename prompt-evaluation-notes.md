# Prompt Evaluation Notes

## Purpose

These notes document observations from evaluating AI-generated outputs for instruction adherence, reasoning quality, safety, and usefulness.

## Evaluation Dimensions

### 1. Instruction Adherence
Does the model follow the user’s actual request, or does it get distracted by irrelevant or conflicting context?

### 2. Reasoning Quality
Does the response explain its reasoning clearly enough for a human reviewer to validate?

### 3. Contextual Relevance
Does the output fit the user’s environment, risk level, and decision context?

### 4. Safety and Boundaries
Does the model avoid unsafe actions, unsupported claims, or overconfident recommendations?

### 5. Human Usefulness
Does the response help a human make a better decision, or does it create false confidence?

## Reflection

The most important issue in enterprise AI adoption may not be whether AI can produce useful outputs. It is whether organizations understand when to trust those outputs, when to challenge them, and how to build workflows that preserve human accountability.
